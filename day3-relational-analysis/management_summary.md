**Executive Summary**
Looking at the sales data shows that most of the money comes from just a few places. The business depends mostly on selling "Computers" and on buyers in the Greater Accra and Ashanti areas. A few top buyers bring in a lot of money, but overall sales started to slow down later in the year. Because of this, the business needs to keep its best buyers happy and start selling different types of items so it does not rely on just one thing.

**5 Key Findings**
*   **Top Buyers Bring in the Most Money:** Just 20% of the buyers make up almost half (46%) of all the sales money. The biggest buyers are Priscilla Owusu and Daniel Osei.
*   **Top Areas:** Most of the money (about 63%) comes from just two areas: Greater Accra and Ashanti.
*   **Best Item:** The "Performance Laptop" is the most popular item in five out of the seven areas.
*   **Computers Rule:** Selling computers made almost 70% of all the money for the business.
*   **Everyone Bought Something:** Every person listed in the database has bought at least one thing; there are no empty customer accounts.

**3 Business Recommendations**
*   **Sell Different Items:** Since almost 70% of the money comes from computers, the business should try selling more small items like bags, mousepads, or storage drives. If computers get hard to find or too expensive, these other items can still make money.
*   **Advertise in Other Areas:** Areas like Central, Northern, Eastern, and Volta bring in less than 30% of the money combined. The business should put up ads or offer special deals to get more people in those areas to buy things.
*   **Reward Top Buyers:** Since a few people buy so much, the business should give them special rewards or discounts. This will make them feel special and want to stay and keep buying from the business.
