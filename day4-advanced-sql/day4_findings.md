# Day 4 Business Findings

The Day 4 analysis uses the `customers`, `products` and `orders` tables.

## 1. Total sales revenue

The 1,200 orders generated **GH₵4,731,304.02** in total revenue.

This gives management the full value of sales in the dataset.

## 2. The strongest month was November 2025

**November 2025** generated **GH₵368,458.32**, which was the highest monthly revenue.

The lowest month was **April 2026**, with **GH₵142,940.52**.

This shows that monthly revenue changed a lot during the period.

## 3. Computers made the largest share of revenue

The **Computers** category generated **GH₵3,275,923.88**, which was about **69.24%** of total revenue.

This means most of the company's revenue in this dataset came from computer products.

## 4. Performance Laptop led the Computers category

The **Performance Laptop** generated **GH₵1,452,562.16**, making it the highest-revenue product in the Computers category.

The **Office Laptop** was next with **GH₵992,991.13**.

This shows that laptop products were very important to the Computers category.

## 5. Greater Accra had the highest regional revenue

**Greater Accra** generated **GH₵1,558,419.20**, the highest revenue among the regions.

**Ashanti** was second with **GH₵1,422,383.92**.

The regional average was **GH₵675,900.57**. Central, Eastern, Northern, Volta and Western were below this average.

This is a performance comparison.

## 6. Priscilla Owusu was the top customer in Ashanti

**Priscilla Owusu (C0083)** spent **GH₵143,462.35**, making her the highest-spending customer in Ashanti.

The top customer in Greater Accra was **Daniel Osei (C0111)** with **GH₵129,150.74**.

The top-three query helps management identify important customers inside each region.

## 7. Customer value groups

The average customer spend was **GH₵26,285.02**.

For this analysis:
- **High value** means at least 150% of average customer spend.
- **Medium value** means at least 75% but less than 150% of average.
- **Low value** means less than 75% of average.

Using this rule, there were **40 High**, **60 Medium** and **80 Low** value customers.

## 8. Month-on-month performance moved up and down

The largest month-on-month increase was from **May 2025 to June 2025**, when revenue increased by about **61.97%**.

There were also months where revenue fell compared with the previous month.
